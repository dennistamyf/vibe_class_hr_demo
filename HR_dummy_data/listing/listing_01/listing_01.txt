Job Listing: Growth Marketing Manager
Company: Northstar Metrics
Location: Remote within the United States, with optional quarterly team weeks in Chicago
Employment: Full-time

About Northstar Metrics
Northstar Metrics is a fictional B2B SaaS company that helps finance and operations teams understand recurring revenue, forecast demand, and act on customer health signals. We are moving from founder-led growth into a repeatable, measurable go-to-market motion.

The role
We are hiring a Growth Marketing Manager to own the experiments and programs that move prospective customers from first touch to qualified pipeline and product activation. You will work closely with Sales, Product Marketing, Product, and Revenue Operations. This is a hands-on role for someone who can set a direction, launch the work, and use evidence to decide what happens next.

Responsibilities
- Build and improve a full-funnel growth plan across paid search, paid social, content, landing pages, webinars, and lifecycle programs.
- Manage channel testing and a quarterly demand-generation budget of approximately $500,000.
- Partner with Revenue Operations to define funnel stages, attribution rules, dashboards, and a reliable weekly performance rhythm.
- Design experiments that improve qualified pipeline, conversion, activation, and efficient customer acquisition rather than optimizing clicks alone.
- Collaborate with Sales on account priorities, campaign follow-up, lead quality, and feedback from the field.
- Write clear briefs and performance readouts that help non-marketers make decisions.

Required experience
- Five or more years in growth, demand generation, lifecycle, or performance marketing, preferably for a B2B SaaS or technology company.
- Direct experience managing paid acquisition, conversion-rate testing, and marketing automation or CRM programs.
- Comfort working with funnel metrics such as CAC, pipeline contribution, conversion rate, activation, and retention.
- A track record of running structured experiments and explaining what the results do and do not prove.
- Strong cross-functional communication and the ability to manage several workstreams without losing detail.

Preferred experience
- Experience with HubSpot or Marketo, Salesforce, Google Analytics 4, LinkedIn Campaign Manager, and a reporting tool such as Looker Studio or Tableau.
- Experience marketing to finance, operations, or other analytical business buyers.
- Familiarity with account-based marketing and sales-assisted buying journeys.

How success will be measured
In the first six months, success will mean a clearer measurement framework, a prioritized experiment backlog, improved lead-to-opportunity conversion, and efficient qualified pipeline growth from at least two repeatable channels.
