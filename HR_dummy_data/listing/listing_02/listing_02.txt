Job Listing: Brand Marketing Manager
Company: Hearthline Home
Location: Hybrid in Portland, Oregon, with flexibility for remote work in Pacific or Mountain time
Employment: Full-time

About Hearthline Home
Hearthline Home is a fictional direct-to-consumer home goods company making durable, repairable products for smaller homes. We are refreshing the brand for a broader audience while keeping the practical, warm point of view that made our early community loyal.

The role
We are hiring a Brand Marketing Manager to turn customer insight and brand strategy into clear, memorable campaigns across web, retail partnerships, email, social, and real-world experiences. You will work with Creative, Merchandising, E-commerce, Customer Care, and outside partners to make the brand recognizable and useful wherever customers meet it.

Responsibilities
- Maintain the brand positioning, messaging architecture, audience narratives, and campaign briefs used across the business.
- Lead integrated seasonal campaigns from insight and concept through launch, channel adaptation, and retrospective.
- Partner with Creative on visual and verbal direction while protecting a coherent, distinctive customer experience.
- Plan customer research, social listening, and competitive reviews to inform decisions about audience and product storytelling.
- Build a practical brand measurement approach using awareness, consideration, engagement, repeat purchase, and qualitative signals.
- Coordinate agencies, freelancers, and internal stakeholders against clear scopes, timelines, and review points.
- Translate customer and business priorities into accessible presentations for senior leadership.

Required experience
- Six or more years in brand marketing, integrated marketing, campaign strategy, or a closely related role.
- Experience developing positioning and campaign platforms from customer or market insight.
- A portfolio of integrated work spanning at least three channels, with a clear account of the candidate's contribution.
- Strong collaboration with creative teams and the judgment to give useful, specific feedback.
- Comfort combining qualitative research, performance data, and business context when making brand decisions.
- Excellent writing, presentation, and project-management skills.

Preferred experience
- Experience in consumer goods, retail, home, lifestyle, or another category where product experience and trust matter.
- Experience managing agency relationships and campaign budgets.
- Familiarity with customer research tools, social listening, brand tracking, and e-commerce analytics.

How success will be measured
In the first six months, success will mean an aligned messaging system, a strong seasonal campaign plan, clearer customer insight practices, and consistent brand execution that improves both customer recognition and commercial performance.
